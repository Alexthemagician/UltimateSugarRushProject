# Café replayability: proposal for discussion

My recommendation is to keep ingredient maps repeatable, offer a daily first-win bonus later, and make recipes/customer orders the reason to return. Do not start with a once-per-day map lock. That restriction can stop a player who is enjoying the game or needs a particular ingredient, especially when recipes combine several regions.

An unlimited ingredient map is a reasonable optional mode, but it needs variety: rotating board layouts, changing objectives, occasional ingredient choices, and a visible next reward. Repeating an identical board merely to refill inventory will feel like work. Let players choose the region or ingredient family they are working toward.

Energy could eventually pace rewards, but it should earn its place through playtesting. If energy is added, I would retain a free practice/replay option and a predictable free refill. Rewarded ads could provide an optional extra reward or refill, with a clear choice before showing an ad. Never make an ad or purchase the only way to obtain a recipe ingredient. A premium cash system also needs defined things to buy; an extra balance without satisfying uses adds clutter.

The current prototype grants three ingredient stacks of 2–4 items per successful board, plus 90–200 coins and 35–80 XP depending on region/stage. Each region contains six ingredients and alternates its two groups independently. Recipes consume 4–5 ingredients, grant 20 XP, and now produce finished batches of 12–50 products. Customers buy those products for 4–18 coins each. These are prototype values, not a validated economy. Boards and customer sales both create coins, while the café currently has no meaningful coin sink; that needs attention before selling currency. Cosmetic furniture, café upgrades and decoration choices are possible sinks, provided they make the café more rewarding to build.

Suggested order for a later experiment:

1. Keep unlimited replays; add varied repeatable challenges and ingredient targeting.
2. Give coins a satisfying purpose through café decoration/upgrades.
3. Trial a daily first-win ingredient bonus without locking other attempts.
4. Measure session length, repeat visits, unfinished recipes, time to required ingredients and reasons for quitting.
5. Only then compare an optional energy/reward pacing model, with free recovery and an ad-free path through ordinary progress.

This can be sustainable as a game loop if serving recipes visibly changes the café and players have meaningful choices. Ads and energy alone will not create that appeal. Financial sustainability is unproven: it would depend on retention, player acquisition cost, voluntary spending/ad engagement and operating costs. We should validate enjoyment before adding monetization constraints.

Nothing in this proposal has been implemented: no daily play cap, energy balance, ad SDK, premium cash balance/icon, purchase flow or recharge timer. The next design decision is whether to test repeatable maps plus a daily bonus first, or explore a separate optional energy mode after the café has meaningful upgrades.
